## Calculation

1. ideal induced power per rotor

$$P_{ideal} = \frac{T^{3/2}}{\sqrt{2\rho A}}$$

$$=  \frac{16.03^{3/2}}{\sqrt{2\times 1.225 \times 0.1464}} = 107.1\ \text{W}$$

2. electrical hover power

$$P_{hover} = \frac{n\,P_{ideal}\,k_{ctrl}}{FM\,\eta}$$

$$=  \frac{4\,\times 107.1\,\times 1.1}{0.5166} = 912.5\ \text{W}$$
