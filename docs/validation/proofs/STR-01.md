## Calculation

1. second moment of area

$$I = \frac{\pi}{64}(D^4 - d^4)$$

$$=  \frac{\pi}{64}(0.0254^4 - 0.02362^4) = 5.15\times 10^{-9}\ \text{m⁴}$$

2. root bending stress, beam theory

$$\sigma = \frac{P L\,(D/2)}{I}$$

$$=  \frac{30.69 \times 0.4883\,(0.0254/2)}{5.15\times 10^{-9}} = 36.98\ \text{MPa}$$
