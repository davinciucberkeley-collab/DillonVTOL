## Calculation

1. motor outer area

$$A = \pi D H + 2\frac{\pi D^2}{4}$$

$$=  \pi 0.048 \times 0.032 + 2\frac{\pi 0.048^2}{4} = 0.008445\ \text{m²}$$

2. steady temperature, lumped

$$T = T_{amb} + \frac{Q}{hA}$$

$$=  308.1 + \frac{45.79}{hA} = 143.4\ \text{°C}$$
