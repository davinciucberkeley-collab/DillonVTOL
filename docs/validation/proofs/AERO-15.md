$$q = \tfrac12 \rho V^2$$

$$=  \tfrac12 \times 1.225 \times 18^2 = 198.5\ \text{Pa}$$

2. disc area

$$A = \pi D_p^2/4$$

$$=  \pi 0.3556^2/4 = 0.09931\ \text{m²}$$

3. momentum-theory upper bound on propeller efficiency

$$\eta_{ideal} = \frac{2}{1 + \sqrt{1 + T/(qA)}}$$

$$=  \frac{2}{1 + \sqrt{1 + 7.434/(qA)}} = 0.9202$$
